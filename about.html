About Parent Teacher Learning Center

Welcome to PTLC. We are glad that you've found us. You've come to the right place if you are looking for training and resources that will help you strengthen and create strong, loving relationships with your children—at home or in the classroom.

Our Mission

At Parent Teacher Learning Center, we believe it takes a community to raise a child. Our mission is to equip educators, parents, and caregivers with the knowledge, skills, and resources they need to nurture confident, independent, and compassionate children.

We do this by offering internationally recognised Montessori training, continued professional development, parenting workshops, and children's leadership programmes—all grounded in respect for the child and the science of human development.

"The education of even a small child, therefore, does not aim at preparing him for school, but for life."

— Maria Montessori

What We Offer

PTLC serves three key communities: educators, parents, and children.

For Educators

  • Internationally recognised Montessori MACTE-accredited programmes for teachers and school administration
  • Continued Professional and Personal Development courses
  • Articles related to teaching, child development, health, and learning
  • Activities for self-care
  • Lesson plan ideas and activities

For Parents

  • Workshops, webinars, and talks on parenting
  • Parenting articles
  • Topics on the deeper issues of marriage
  • Parent/child bonding activities
  • Activities for self-care
  • Resources that augment your child's learning outside the classroom

For Children

  • Training that harnesses leadership skills, creative thinking, and problem solving
  • Activities that instil global citizenship and civic mindedness
  • Activities that promote healthy minds and lifestyles for total well-being
  • Materials and resources for enhanced learning

Our Partnership with CGMS

PTLC is proud to partner with the Center for Guided Montessori Studies (CGMS), a leader in blended Montessori teacher and leadership training since 2006.

CGMS offers MACTE-accredited, IMC-recognised programmes with flexible online learning, hands-on experience, and expert mentorship. Designed by respected Montessorians Kitty Bravo, Tim Seldin, and Jonathan Wolff, CGMS uses cutting-edge distance-learning technologies to make quality Montessori training accessible worldwide.

Through this partnership, PTLC brings internationally recognised Montessori certification and professional development to Singapore and the wider Asian region.

CGMS at a Glance

  • Founded in 2006
  • MACTE-accredited programmes
  • IMC-recognised certifications
  • Over 400 hours of video lessons
  • Flexible online + residency format
  • Serves students across Asia and worldwide

Accreditations & Recognition

Our programmes meet the highest international standards for Montessori teacher education.

MACTE Accreditation

The Montessori Accreditation Council for Teacher Education (MACTE) is recognised by the US Department of Education as the accrediting agency for Montessori teacher education programmes. Our full certification programmes meet MACTE's Essential Standards and Criteria.

Learn more about MACTE →

International Montessori Council (IMC)

CGMS is affiliated with the International Montessori Council. Graduates of our full certification programmes receive an IMC teaching certificate, recognised by Montessori schools worldwide.
