Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our Montessori programmes and admissions.

Montessori is a child-centered educational approach developed by Dr. Maria Montessori. It emphasises hands-on learning, self-directed activity, and collaborative play. Children make creative choices in their learning while teachers offer age-appropriate activities to guide the process. The goal is to nurture independence, confidence, and a lifelong love of learning.

Professional development courses award CGMS Professional Development Certificates.

Our courses cater to a wide audience: Montessori assistants, volunteers, substitutes, and support staff; experienced certified teachers seeking refresher training; school administrators and aspiring school heads; parents who wish to deepen their understanding of child development; and anyone passionate about the Montessori philosophy.

Graduates of our professional development courses (such as Montessori Fundamentals and English Language) receive a CGMS Professional Development Certificate. Graduates of full certification programmes receive an International Montessori Council (IMC) teaching certificate, with MACTE accreditation for full certification tiers.

People Also Ask

Common questions people search for when exploring Montessori training in Singapore.

How long does Montessori teacher training take in Singapore?

Professional development courses at PTLC typically run for 10 weeks. Full teacher certification programmes through our CGMS partnership span 18-19 months, including distance learning, a residential session, and a 9-month practicum.

Is Montessori certification worth it?

Yes. MACTE-accredited Montessori certification is internationally recognised and opens doors to teaching positions in authentic Montessori schools worldwide. It provides deep pedagogical knowledge that benefits both new and experienced educators.

What is the difference between Montessori and traditional preschool?

Montessori emphasises self-directed learning, mixed-age classrooms, hands-on materials, and uninterrupted work cycles. Teachers act as guides rather than lecturers, fostering independence and intrinsic motivation in children.

Can parents use Montessori methods at home?

Absolutely. PTLC offers parenting workshops and resources that teach Montessori principles for the home environment, including creating child-accessible spaces, encouraging practical life skills, and supporting your child's natural development.
