Admissions & Enrolment

Ready to begin your Montessori journey? Here's everything you need to know about applying for our teacher training, professional development, and parenting programmes.

How to Apply

Our admissions process is straightforward. Follow these steps to enrol in your chosen programme.

  1. 1

    Explore Our Programmes

    Review our programme listings to find the course that matches your goals—whether you are an educator seeking certification, a parent looking for workshops, or a school leader aiming for administration training.

  2. 2

    Contact Us to Express Interest

    Call +65 8204 5061 or email catherine@ptlc.com.sg. We will answer your questions, confirm programme availability, and guide you toward the right certification track.

  3. 3

    Complete Your Application

    Submit the required application form and supporting documents. For the School Leadership Program, this includes the application fee of S$150. For certification programmes, you may need to provide proof of prior education.

  4. 4

    Receive Confirmation & Payment Instructions

    Once your application is reviewed, we will send you an enrolment confirmation with payment instructions. Full payment or an agreed payment plan secures your place in the cohort.

  5. 5

    Begin Your Learning Journey

    Access your course materials, join the online learning platform, and attend your first weekly live chat. Your Instructional Guide will support you every step of the way.

Entry Requirements

Who can apply for our programmes? Here's a quick guide.

Professional Development Courses

  • Open to Montessori assistants, volunteers, substitutes, and support staff
  • Suitable for experienced certified teachers seeking refresher training
  • No formal academic prerequisites
  • Basic computer skills and reliable internet connection required
  • Access to a Montessori classroom is helpful for observation assignments

Full Certification Programmes

  • Full Certification: Minimum Bachelor's degree (or international equivalent)
  • Provisional Certification: High school diploma with educational portfolio
  • Must complete distance learning, residency, and 9-month practicum
  • Practicum requires placement in an approved Montessori classroom
  • Commitment of 10-15 study hours per week
