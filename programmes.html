Programme FAQs

Specific questions about enrolling in our Montessori courses and certifications.

The Full Certification programme includes the online distance-learning component plus one or two short on-site residencies, and is MACTE accredited. The Associate Certificate covers the same content but does not include the residential component and is not MACTE accredited. We recommend the Full Certification for the highest quality of education and international recognition.

The School Leadership Program is designed for current and aspiring school administrators. It has components tailored for those with more Montessori experience than business, as well as for others who are less familiar with Montessori pedagogy than they are with the business side. Contact us to discuss which track suits your background.

For professional development courses, plan approximately 5 hours per week. For full teacher certification programmes, adult learners should plan 10-15 hours per week to view video lectures, complete assignments, and participate in discussions and live chats.

Yes. Our programmes are designed for working professionals. The flexible online format allows you to schedule study at your own convenience. Weekly live chats are scheduled in advance, and all video lectures and materials are accessible on-demand.

Professional development courses are priced per programme (S$520-S$670). Contact us for payment plans and available instalment options.

Montessori Fundamentals Early Childhood

A 10-week guided course combining our popular Classroom Leadership module with a comprehensive Early Childhood overview.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Montessori Philosophy and key concepts
  • Qualities of the prepared environment
  • How to start the school year successfully
  • Positive discipline and classroom management
  • Classroom roles and leadership
  • Early Childhood curriculum areas: Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Mathematics, Cultural and Science
  • Video lectures on purpose, best practices, and lesson presentations for each area

Who Should Apply

  • Montessori assistants, volunteers, substitutes, and support staff
  • Experienced certified teachers seeking a refresher
  • Anyone new to Montessori who wants a solid foundation in Early Childhood (ages 3-6)

School Leadership Program

A comprehensive training programme for school administrators, designed in collaboration with the Montessori Foundation.

Programme Overview

This programme gives school heads everything they need to manage successful, authentic, and long-lived Montessori schools. It blends the best of online learning with a single, short on-site residency.

Two Tracks Available

  • For Montessori-experienced leaders: Components tailored for those with more Montessori experience than business knowledge.
  • For business-experienced leaders: Components for those less familiar with Montessori pedagogy than with the business side.

Montessori English Language Course

A 10-week guided course exploring language development in early childhood through the Montessori lens.

What You Will Learn

  • The natural phenomena of language development in early childhood
  • The child's sensitivity and fascination with language
  • Oral expression and age-appropriate visual and auditory perceptual experiences
  • Vocabulary development and enrichment
  • Phonemic awareness and word study
  • Beginning handwriting and expressive writing
  • Lessons for teaching simple grammar (function of words)
  • Creating a language-rich environment for emergent literacy
